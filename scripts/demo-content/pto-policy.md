# Time Off Policy

Twenty-five days of paid time off per year, plus public holidays. Days accrue
monthly and up to five unused days carry into the next year.

## Requesting time off

There is no approval threshold for three days or fewer — post it in your team
channel and add it to the shared calendar. Anything longer than a week should be
raised with your manager a month ahead so coverage can be arranged.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is not counted against PTO and is not capped. Do not work while sick,
and do not apologise for being ill.

## On-call interaction

Being paged during PTO should not happen. If it does, the day is refunded and
the escalation policy in [[Incident Response Runbook]] gets a look at why the
rotation failed.
