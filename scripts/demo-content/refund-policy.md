# Refund Policy

Refunds inside 30 days of purchase are approved without escalation. Say yes and
move on — the argument costs more than the refund.

## Decision table

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Within 30 days, any reason | Refund in full, no questions |
| 30–90 days, unused | Refund in full |
| 30–90 days, used | Prorated credit |
| Over 90 days | Escalate to a team lead |
| Chargeback already filed | Do not refund; billing handles it |

## Processing

Refunds land back on the original payment method in 5–10 business days. Tell the
customer the range, not the midpoint — under-promising here prevents a second
ticket asking where the money is.

Duplicate charges are always refunded in full regardless of age.
